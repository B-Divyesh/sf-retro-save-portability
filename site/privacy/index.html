Policy · 6 September 2026

How your data is handled.

The app processes save files on your device. It does not upload or track them.

Data on your device

Folder paths, file names, hashes, manifests, save contents, device labels, and transfer history stay on your device.

The free tools need no account. Removing the app does not delete bundles you created.

License verification

When you add a Keeper license, the app stores its token on your device.

It sends only that token to the Sociobot billing API. A valid result is cached for up to one day.

Sociobot/Dodo processes purchases as merchant of record. Retro Save Portability does not receive your full card details.

Website requests

The website has no advertising, analytics, tracking pixels, third-party fonts, or third-party scripts.

GitHub serves release details and downloads. GitHub may receive your IP address and browser details under its policy.

Your choices

All save-preservation and safety tools work without Keeper. Remove stored web licenses by clearing this site’s browser data.

Remove the desktop license from the Keeper panel. Email support@sociobot.in for billing-data help.

Changes

Material changes will be dated here. Local save handling will not silently become a cloud service.

Move legally owned game saves between computers and emulators.
